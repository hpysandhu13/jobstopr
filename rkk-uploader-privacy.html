Privacy Policy — Raat Ki Kahani Uploader

Effective date: October 19, 2026

What this app is

Raat Ki Kahani Uploader is a personal automation tool created and used solely by the owner of the YouTube channel "Raat Ki Kahani" (@raatkikahani-raazzkibaat). It connects to the owner's own YouTube account through the YouTube Data API so the owner can upload and manage videos, including setting thumbnails, on his own channel.

What data the app accesses

The only Google user data this app accesses is the owner's own YouTube channel data, and only to the extent needed to upload videos and set thumbnails on that channel. The app is used by one person — the channel owner — and has no other users.

What the app stores

The app stores OAuth access and refresh tokens only on the owner's own computer, so it can stay signed in to the owner's channel. No other personal data is stored.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal data from any third party.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share any personal data with any third party.
  • It does not track users.

Revoking access

The owner may revoke the app's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
